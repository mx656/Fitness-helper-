Daily Wellness Tips
💧

Hydrate First

Drink 500ml of water within 30 minutes of waking to kick-start metabolism and mental clarity.

🥗

Protein Every Meal

Aim for 20–30g of protein per meal to preserve muscle, reduce hunger, and stabilise blood sugar.

🚶

10,000 Steps

Daily walking reduces cardiovascular risk by up to 30%. Break it up across the day if needed.

📵

Screen Curfew

Avoid screens 1 hour before bed. Blue light suppresses melatonin and disrupts deep sleep.

🧂

Watch Sodium

Keep sodium under 2,300mg/day. Excess salt causes bloating and raises blood pressure over time.

🫁

Box Breathing

4 counts in → hold 4 → out 4 → hold 4. Repeat 4 times to instantly lower stress levels.
