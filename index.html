Quick wins
Daily Wellness Tips
💧

Hydrate on Waking

Drink 500ml of water within 30 minutes of waking. Research shows even mild dehydration (1–2%) impairs cognitive performance.

🥗

Protein First

Aim for 20–30g of protein per meal. It has the highest satiety effect of all macronutrients, reducing total daily calorie intake.

🚶

Daily Steps

A 2023 JAMA study found that 8,000 steps per day reduces all-cause mortality risk by 51% compared to 4,000 steps.

📵

Screen Curfew

Avoid screens 60–90 minutes before bed. Blue light delays melatonin onset by up to 3 hours, reducing sleep quality.

🧂

Watch Sodium

The WHO recommends under 2,000mg of sodium daily. Most people consume double this, raising blood pressure and cardiovascular risk.

🫁

Box Breathing

Inhale 4 counts → hold 4 → exhale 4 → hold 4. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system, lowering cortisol within minutes.
